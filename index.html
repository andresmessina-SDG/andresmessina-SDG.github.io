S.D.G.

Soli Deo Gloria · To God alone be the glory

All five are free. None of them asks for an account or tracks you.

Bach headed his scores J.J., “Jesus, help,” and signed them S.D.G., “to God alone the glory.” This page borrows his habit, and its staves carry the first and last lines of his setting of Luther’s Ein feste Burg ist unser Gott, BWV 302. His name is a tune as well: in German, B is B flat and H is B natural.
